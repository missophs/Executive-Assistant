## 1. "Tell me about a time you led HR through an acquisition or integration."
Use: Cprime (main story)
- **Situation:** Global Head of People, 1,000+ employees in the U.S., EMEA, India and Australia, PE-backed (Goldman Sachs/Everstone), buying four companies and then being sold. Attrition was 31%.
- **Task:** keep the leaders we were buying, integrate faster, clear buyer diligence, and still run HR.
- **Action:** led People diligence, Day 1 organization design, decision rights and critical-talent retention on every deal. Realigned roles, reporting lines, pay and retention terms. Put global records into one HR system and moved India to an employer-of-record.
- **Result:** integration 30% faster, 100% of target leaders retained, attrition from 31% to near zero, 100% of diligence items cleared.
- **Tie back:** "That is your M&A integration line, run across the same regions."
